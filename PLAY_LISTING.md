## Full Description (max 4000 characters)

Unwind your mind with ZenSlide — a sleek, calming offline sliding block puzzle game engineered to provide deep mental stimulation without rush, stress, or distractions.

Your mission is simple yet satisfying: navigate a 6x6 puzzle grid by sliding horizontal blocks left and right, and vertical blocks up and down, to clear a direct escape route for the glowing crimson key block to pass through the exit portal.

With 40 handcrafted and BFS-solver-verified levels, ZenSlide tests your spatial awareness, forward planning, and problem-solving skills across four balanced difficulty tiers.

Whether taking a quick break on your daily commute or winding down before sleep, ZenSlide offers an ad-free, completely private offline sanctuary.

### 🌟 Game Highlights:

- **100% Offline Gameplay**: Play freely anywhere — on flights, trains, or remote locations without requiring Wi-Fi or mobile data.
- **40 Curated Level Layouts**: Progress through 4 difficulty packs designed to challenge and delight:
  • Starter: Intuitive warm-ups to master sliding mechanics
  • Classic: Balanced grid puzzles demanding strategic block maneuvers
  • Challenger: Intricate multi-block barriers requiring smart reverse-planning
  • Master: Advanced puzzle layouts for true spatial masters
- **Tactile Procedural Audio**: Enjoy realistic wooden and stone block sliding sound effects synthesized dynamically using the Web Audio API, complemented by pleasant exit chimes and victory fanfares.
- **Optimal Shortest-Path Scoring**: Every level is evaluated by a real-time Breadth-First Search (BFS) graph solver. Match the minimum move target to earn the prestigious 3-star rating!
- **Interactive Hint Assistant**: Stuck on a tricky barrier? Tap the Hint button to receive an instant, animated guide for the optimal next move.
- **Unlimited Undo & Instant Restart**: Explore alternative pathways with unlimited move take-backs and rapid board resets.
- **Minimalist Modern Dark Aesthetic**: Battery-saving dark background with vibrant neon accent blocks and smooth animations.
- **Zero Ads, Zero Trackers**: Enjoy pure uninterrupted gameplay with no ads, popups, or microtransactions.
